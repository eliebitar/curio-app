Your privacy.

Curio stores your collection on your device. There is no account, advertising, tracking, or developer-operated sync service in this version.

Effective 25 October 2026. This policy covers Curio: My Collection Shelf for iPhone and iPad, developed by ELIE BITAR.

Information stored on your device

Collection and item names, notes, photos, quantities, editions, conditions, dates, achievements, and profile preferences are saved locally. Curio does not transmit this library to the developer or to analytics services.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph an item and grant permission. The system photo picker lets you select an image for an item. Curio stores the selected image with that item; it does not upload it to an image recognition service.

Backups and sharing

If you export a backup, Curio creates a JSON file containing your library, photos, profile, and achievements. You choose its destination in the system file picker. Any cloud or sharing service you select handles that copy under its own policies. Curio does not encrypt the exported file. Apple device backups may include local app data depending on your settings.

Data collection and third parties

Curio contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs and has no account backend. The developer does not receive your collection data through the app. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information under its privacy statement. We do not add website analytics or advertising cookies.

Support messages

If you email support, the developer receives the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send, and uses them to answer your request. These messages are separate from your on-device collection.

Deleting your data

Delete individual items or collections inside Curio. Removing the app removes its local data from that installation. Copies you exported or included in device backups must be managed separately in the service or location where you saved them.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app’s practices change. For privacy questions or support-message deletion requests, contact elie.bitar@eb-net.org.
