Questions about Curio? Start here, or email support.

How do I start a collection?

Tap + in Collections, enter a name, choose a category, and create it. Use an open-ended collection for anything that grows, or enable “Track a complete set” and enter one item per line.

How do I record items I own?

Open a collection and tap +. Give the item a name and leave “I own this” enabled. Add a photo, edition, condition, quantity, date, or note if you like. Items with ownership switched off remain on your to-find list.

How do completion achievements work?

A checklist is complete when every listed item is owned. Open-ended collections do not have a completion target. Achievements stay earned even if you later change a checklist.

Can Curio identify a figure from a photo?

Photos document your items. This version suggests a small number of editable starter lists by collection name; it does not identify exact products from pictures or search a complete product catalog.

How do I back up or move my collection?

In Profile, choose Export backup, then save the JSON file in Files. It includes your photos, profile, collections, and achievements. On another device with Curio, choose Restore a backup. Existing items with newer local edits are kept. Backup files are not encrypted by Curio.

Does Curio sync or need an account?

No account is needed. This version stores data locally. Export a backup before deleting the app or moving devices. Automatic account-based sync is not included.

Why is the camera unavailable?

Allow camera access in the device’s Settings. You can also add an image with the photo picker. A camera is required for taking a new photograph.

What should I send support?

Include your device model, iOS version, and the steps that caused the problem. Please avoid sending private photos or a full library backup unless needed for your request.
