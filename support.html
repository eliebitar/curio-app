Questions about Curio? Start here, or email support.

How do I start a collection?

Tap + in Collections, then Browse templates to search 29 ready-made lists. Preview a set, choose Use this template, and tick what you own. You can also enter a name and category to create anything from scratch. Use an open-ended collection for anything that grows, or enable “Track a complete set” and enter one item per line.

How do I record items I own?

Open a collection and tap +. Give the item a name and leave “I own this” enabled. Add a photo, edition, condition, quantity, date, or note if you like. Items with ownership switched off remain on your to-find list.

How do completion achievements work?

A checklist is complete when every listed item is owned. Open-ended collections do not have a completion target. Achievements stay earned even if you later change a checklist.

Can Curio identify a figure from a photo?

Photos document your items. Curio includes 29 scoped, editable templates with 494 named items and original reference illustrations. It does not identify exact products from pictures or search a comprehensive remote product catalog.

How do I back up or move my collection?

For automatic sync, use the same Apple Account on each iPhone or iPad, enable iCloud for Curio, and allow time for changes to transfer. To keep a separate snapshot, in Profile choose Export backup, then save the JSON file in Files. It includes your photos, profile, collections, and achievements. On another device with Curio, choose Restore a backup. Existing items with newer local edits are kept. Backup files are not encrypted by Curio.

Does Curio sync or need an account?

No separate Curio account is needed. Collections, photos, and achievements sync using your private iCloud database; profile preferences also use iCloud. Use the same Apple Account and ensure iCloud has available storage. The app saves locally while offline or signed out. Profile shows iCloud status and recovery guidance. Sync mirrors deletions, so keep exported backups for historical snapshots. iPhone and iPad are supported; there is no Android, Windows, or web app.

Why have my changes not appeared on my other device?

Check that both devices use the same Apple Account, iCloud is enabled for Curio, and there is enough iCloud storage. Open Curio on both devices and allow time for changes to transfer. Profile shows sync status and the last successful transfer. If you are offline, edits remain saved on the device until iCloud can sync them. Export a backup before changing accounts or removing the app.

Why is the camera unavailable?

Allow camera access in the device’s Settings. In an item’s Edit screen, choose Photo library to select an existing image, or Take photo to capture one. A personal photo replaces its reference illustration; removing it reveals the illustration again. A camera is required for taking a new photograph.

What should I send support?

Include your device model, iOS version, and the steps that caused the problem. Please avoid sending private photos or a full library backup unless needed for your request.
